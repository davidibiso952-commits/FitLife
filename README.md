# 💪 FitLife
### Your Personal Trainer

FitLife is a personal fitness
application designed to help users
build
healthy exercise habits, stay motivated, and manage their fitness journey based on their weight in a simple and accessible way.

_ _ _

## Problem Being Solved

Many people struggle to maintain consistent fitness routine and engage in the wrong weight loss procedures because they
may not Know which exercises to
do, how to organize their workouts,the right weight loss procedures to engage in, and how to stay motivated.

FitLife aims to make personal
fitness guidance more accessible
by providing
useful workout tools and guidance together in one simple
application.

_ _ _

## 💡 Solution

FitLife provides users with a
simple digital personal trainer
experience.

The app helps users access fitness
information, follow workout
activities,
set fitness goals, and stay
motivated throughout their fitness
journey.

_ _ _

## ✨ Main Features

- 🏋️ Workout and exercise guidance
- 🎯 Fitness goals
- 📊 Fitness progress tracking
- 🗓️ Workout planning
- 💡 Exercise information
- 👤 Personalized fitness
experience
- 📱 Simple and user-friendly
interface

## 📱 How to use the App

1. Open the FitLife application.
2. Navigate through the available
fitness options
3. Select a workout or fitness
activity
4. Follow the instructions
provided by the app
5. Track your progress and
continue working toward your
fitness goals.
